# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and open to correction.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Make space for different disciplines and levels of experience.
- Protect confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and community safety.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[opensource@attractor.fi](mailto:opensource@attractor.fi). Maintainers will
review reports in confidence and may edit or remove content, close
conversations, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. This policy applies in
project spaces and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.
