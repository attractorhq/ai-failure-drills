# Participant brief

You are joining a fictional incident exercise about an AI-enabled product.
There is no hidden test and no single perfect response.

## During the exercise

- Act from your assigned role while bringing your real expertise.
- Say what you would do, who would do it, and what evidence would change the
  decision.
- Consider affected users, not only service health.
- You may ask for information. The facilitator may say it is unavailable.
- Call out uncertainty and disagreement.
- Do not use real customer data, credentials, or confidential incident details.

## What we are practising

The exercise tests the surrounding product system: detection, decisions,
permissions, fallbacks, communication, recovery, and ownership. It is not a
test of whether a model can be made perfect.

At the end, help select a small number of improvements with named owners and
review dates.
